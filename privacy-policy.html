1. Who we are

"Frostforge" (placeholder name) is a mobile game. The data controller is the Frostforge developer, Anderson Fantin. Contact for privacy requests: playfrostforge@gmail.com. The game is for players aged 16 and over.

2. What we collect and why

Data When Purpose Legal basis (GDPR/LGPD)
Anonymous account id (Cognito "guest" user id) On first launch Identify your save so you can play Contract (providing the game)
Game state: base layout, resources, research, heroes, troops, tutorial progress, timestamps While playing Run the game on our server (the server is authoritative) Contract
Google account identifier (no email or profile) (only if you tap "Link Google") When you link Recover your save on a new device Consent (you choose to link)
Device integrity token (Google Play Integrity) At guest sign-up on Android Block fake clients and abuse Legitimate interest (security, anti-cheat)
Technical logs: may include IP address as processed by AWS, request time, endpoint, status, user id Every request Security, debugging, cost control Legitimate interest
Feedback you send from the menu: your text, your user id, time, app version, platform, language When you send feedback Read and answer your feedback Consent
Usage statistics, only if you tap "Allow" (changeable in the menu): per day, counts of events such as tutorial steps, sessions, play time, highest Core level, first research/recruit/expedition, and a short code identifying a script error; with your user id, app version and platform While you play, after you allow it Understand where players get stuck and how many come back Consent

We do not sell your data, show third-party ads, or use your data for profiling. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

Usage statistics are stored as daily totals per player (not one record per action), contain no free text, advertising id or device id, and nothing is sent until you choose "Allow". Native crash reports come from Google Play (Android vitals).

Planned (not collected yet): in-app purchase receipts. Before they start, this policy will be updated and, where required, we will ask for consent.

3. Who receives your data

  • Amazon Web Services (hosting, database, logs, sign-in via Amazon Cognito, email notifications). Processor.
  • Google (Google sign-in if you link; Play Integrity; Google Play for distribution and, in future, payments).
  • Nobody else. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

4. Where data is stored

Amazon Web Services region us-east-2 (Ohio, USA). If you are outside the USA your data is transferred there; we rely on AWS's standard contractual clauses/data protection terms for transfers from the EU/UK/Brazil.

5. How long we keep it

  • Game state and account: until you delete the account.
  • Feedback messages: automatically deleted after 180 days (or earlier when you delete your account). A copy is also emailed to the developer inbox and deleted on request.
  • Usage statistics: automatically deleted after 120 days (or earlier when you delete your account); the record of your choice (Allow / No thanks) and the day you first chose are kept until you delete your account.
  • Technical logs: 30 days (set in AWS CloudWatch).
  • A short deletion marker (about 2 hours) is kept after deletion so a stale session cannot recreate the account.
  • Backups: in production, DynamoDB point-in-time recovery may keep deleted data in backups for up to 35 days.

6. Your rights and how to delete your data

Under GDPR (EU/UK) and LGPD (Brazil) you may ask to access, correct, port, or delete your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD). - Delete everything yourself: in the game open Menu -> Delete account. This removes your save, your link to Google, your sign-in record and your feedback rows immediately (backend DELETE /v1/me). It cannot be undone. - Other requests: email playfrostforge@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. We may need details such as the approximate time of play or a feedback id to find your data, since guest accounts have no name or email. - If you linked Google, unlinking or deleting on Google's side does not delete the game save; use Delete account.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (AWS-managed encryption). Access to the backend is limited to the developer through AWS IAM. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify authorities and affected players of breaches as the law requires.

8. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above. Material changes will be announced in the game.

Não pode ser desfeito. - Outros pedidos: escreva para playfrostforge@gmail.com. Respondemos em até 30 dias. Podemos pedir dados como o horário aproximado de jogo ou o id de um feedback para localizar seus dados, pois contas de convidado não têm nome nem e-mail. - Se você vinculou o Google, desvincular ou excluir no Google não apaga o progresso do jogo; use Excluir conta.

7. Segurança

Os dados são criptografados em trânsito (HTTPS) e em repouso (criptografia gerenciada pela AWS). O acesso ao backend é restrito ao desenvolvedor via AWS IAM. Nenhum sistema é totalmente seguro; comunicaremos incidentes à autoridade e aos jogadores afetados conforme a lei.
